About Me

I am an independent researcher working on generative modeling, learning dynamics, and efficient inference. I hold a master's degree in computer science from Kyoto University.

I am interested in how training objectives shape learned models, and how those models behave when deployed with limited computation. My work combines mathematical analysis with controlled experiments.

Research Interests

Generative Modeling

Flow and diffusion models, distribution transport, and the relationship between training targets and generated distributions.

Learning Dynamics

How changes to supervision are realized during training, and how learning errors affect model behavior.

Efficient Inference

Finite-step generation and the tradeoffs between model quality, numerical integration, and computational cost.
